This lesson doesn't teach anything new. Instead, it puts Lessons 1 to 7 together into one complete computer task, the way you'd actually do it.

The instructions in this task are already clear. Understanding unclear client instructions is a separate skill, covered later in the Beginner VA Journey. Here, the only challenge is doing the work on the computer.

The Task

From the client:

"Hi! Here's a ZIP file with 6 photos of our new mugs. Please rename them in our usual style, product-color-angle.jpg (for example mug-blue-front.jpg), and upload them to the Product Photos page on our website. Please also take a screenshot of the renamed files so I can check them. Thanks!"

Everything you need for this task was covered in this course.

      The Workflow, Step by Step

      Here is the same task again, with the lesson where you learned each step:

      1. Download the ZIP
        It lands in Downloads, or check the browser's download list. (Lesson 4)
      2. Extract it
        Right-click › Extract All on Windows, or double-click on a Mac. Work from the extracted folder, not inside the ZIP. (Lesson 4)
      3. Move it into place
        Move the folder from Downloads into your work folder for this client, so it's organized and easy to find later. (Lesson 3)
      4. Rename the photos
        Right-click › Rename. Follow the client's style exactly, and keep the .jpg at the end. (Lessons 2 and 3)
      5. Screenshot
        Capture just the folder, with nothing private or unrelated in view. (Lesson 2)
      6. Upload
        Type the website's exact address to open the upload page, then upload the renamed photos. (Lessons 4 and 5)
      7. Check
        Wait until the upload finishes, then confirm all 6 photos appear with the right names. (Lesson 4)

      Other orders can work too. What matters is that each step is finished properly before the next one depends on it: you can't rename photos that are still inside a ZIP, and you shouldn't take the screenshot until the names are final.

      Practice

      Try It on Your Own Computer

      Reading about it is not the same as doing it. Use any three photos or files you already have, and do the same workflow for real. Tick each step as you finish it. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

      Key Takeaway
      • Most computer tasks are a chain of simple skills: download, extract, organize, rename, work, save, upload, check.
      • Finish each step properly before the next one depends on it.
      • When a step goes wrong, use STOP → CHECK → TRY → SEARCH → ASK.
      • The best way to make these skills stick is to practice them on your own computer.
