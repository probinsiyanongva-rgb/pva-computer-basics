About this course

Using a computer for VA work

Almost every VA task happens on a computer. Before you can learn email, Google Workspace, or any specialized VA skill, you need to be comfortable with the computer itself.

This course is for you if you are new to computers, you mostly use a phone, or you use a computer but often get stuck with files, downloads, or problems you don't know how to fix.

It works for both Windows laptops and Macs, and for desktop computers too. Where the steps differ, the lessons show both.

By the end of this course, you should be able to:

  • recognize the main parts of a laptop or desktop computer and the everyday words for them
  • control your computer with the mouse, keyboard and windows, including right-clicking and switching between programs
  • create, name, find, copy, move and organize files and folders
  • download and upload files, open ZIP files, and know where your files are stored
  • use a web browser with tabs, web addresses and bookmarks
  • take a screenshot and use the most common keyboard shortcuts
  • work through a computer problem calmly, step by step, instead of panicking
  • keep your computer and accounts safe with simple everyday habits

This course teaches the computer itself. Email, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and sharing files with other people are covered in the next course, Internet, Email & Google Workspace.

The PVA Beginner VA Journey

You are in Stage 2: Build Your Foundation

Stage 2 makes you ready to learn and work online. It has two courses, taken in this order:

  1. Computer & Laptop Basics (this course): the computer itself.
  2. Internet, Email & Google Workspace: working online with email and Google tools.

You don't need to finish VA Foundations first, but it gives you the bigger picture of VA work if you are completely new.
